INTRODUCTION & OVERVIEW

What is a Private Limited Company??

A Private Limited Company (Pvt Ltd) is a separate legal entity incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013. It offers limited liability to its shareholders — meaning personal assets of directors/shareholders are protected from business debts.

With a minimum of 2 directors and 2 shareholders and no minimum paid-up capital requirement, it is the most popular business structure for startups, SMEs, and businesses seeking investment.
