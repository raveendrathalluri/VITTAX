INTRODUCTION & OVERVIEW

What is an LLP?

A Limited Liability Partnership (LLP) is a modern hybrid business structure that combines the flexibility and tax efficiency of a partnership with the limited liability protection of a company. Governed by the Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008, an LLP is a separate legal entity — it can own property, enter contracts, and sue or be sued in its own name.

Unlike a general partnership, partners in an LLP are not personally liable for the wrongful acts of other partners or for the firm's debts beyond their agreed contribution. This makes LLP the preferred structure for professionals, consultants, and service businesses that want liability protection without the full compliance burden of a Pvt Ltd company.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Limited Liability Partnership (LLP) Registration in India.

What is the difference between LLP and Pvt Ltd?

LLP has lower annual compliance (Form 8 + Form 11 vs multiple ROC filings for Pvt Ltd), no mandatory statutory audit below ₹40L turnover, and more flexibility in management. However, Pvt Ltd is better suited for equity funding, venture capital, and ESOPs. Choose LLP for service or professional firms; choose Pvt Ltd if you plan to raise external investment.

Can an LLP receive FDI (Foreign Direct Investment)?

Yes, LLP can receive FDI in most sectors, except those under the restricted list. The process involves filing Form FC-GPR with the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and complying with FEMA regulations. This makes LLPs attractive for foreign partners looking for a stable business structure.

What are the annual compliance requirements for an LLP?

LLPs must file two main forms annually: Form 8 (Statement of Account and Solvency) by October 30 and Form 11 (Annual Return) by May 31. There is no mandatory corporate tax audit unless turnover exceeds ₹40 lakh. Additionally, an LLP Agreement must be filed within 30 days of incorporation.

Can designated partners take a salary from the LLP?

Yes, partners can receive remuneration (salary), profits, or benefits as defined in the LLP Agreement. This amount is treated as an expense for the LLP and is deducted before calculating distributable profits.

Can I open a bank account for my LLP?

Yes, an LLP can open a current bank account in its own name once the Certificate of Incorporation and LLP Agreement are filed. The bank typically requires the LLP PAN, incorporation certificate, and partner IDs.

What is the validity of a DIN?

Once allotted, the Director Identification Number (DIN) is valid for the entire lifetime of the individual and does not expire. However, the DIN must be updated with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) if the director changes their mobile number or email address.
